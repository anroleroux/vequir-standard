The common TP4056 single-cell lithium charger module with battery protection
and a USB-C input: a small board with the TP4056 charger chip, a DW01
protection IC and an 8205A dual MOSFET, red and blue (or green) charge
LEDs, and solder pads: IN+ and IN- beside the USB-C port, B+ and B- for the
cell, and OUT+ and OUT- for the protected output. It charges at up to 1 A
to 4.2 V.
