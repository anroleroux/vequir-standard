A single-cell lithium battery management (protection) board, 3.7 V and 3 A,
by Sunlephant, sold by Mantech (stock code 14M9549, part number 210454). It
is a narrow strip of board, 30.3 mm long, 3.7 mm wide and 2.0 mm thick over
its parts, for one lithium cell, with a nickel-plated solder pad at each end
and its two chips (the 8205A dual MOSFET and the protection IC) between them.
