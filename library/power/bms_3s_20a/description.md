A Li-ion/LiPo 3S cell pack protection module (BMS), as sold by DIY
Electronics. It protects a pack of three 18650 or 26650 lithium cells
(3.6 V to 3.7 V nominal) wired in series. Charge it at 12.6 V and never above
12.75 V. It delivers up to 20 A and 252 W. The board is 64 x 20 x 3.4 mm.

The model follows a photograph of the top of the board. It shows the four
MOSFETs as their own blocks, the small components as a few larger
approximate blocks, and a block over every terminal, so a housing knows
where to stay clear.
