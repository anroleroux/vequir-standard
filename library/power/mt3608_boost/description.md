A boost (step-up) voltage regulator board built on the MT3608, as sold by
MicroRobotics (product code MT3608-2A): input 2 to 24 V, output 2 to 28 V,
1 A continuous and 2 A peak, up to 93 % efficient. Its output must be
higher than its input. A blue multi-turn trimmer sets the output voltage;
VIN+ and VIN- pads are at one end of the board and VOUT+ and VOUT- at the
other.
