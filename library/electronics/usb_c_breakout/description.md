A USB-C breakout board, horizontal, as sold by MicroRobotics (product code
USBC-HOR): a USB 2.0 Type-C receptacle on a small board, its plug going in
parallel to the board, with its pins CC2, SBU1, D+, D-, CC1, SBU2, VBUS and
GND broken out to a row of eight holes at 2.54 mm pitch. It is
20.2 x 14 x 4.8 mm, with two mounting holes in the corners beside the
receptacle.
