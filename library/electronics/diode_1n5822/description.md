A 1N5822 Schottky rectifier: 3 A, 40 V, in a small cylindrical axial
package (DO-201AD) with a lead out of each end and a band at the cathode
end. About 0.35 to 0.45 V forward drop at the few hundred milliamps a small
unit draws. Modelled as mounted on a board: lying flat, its leads bent down
at 0.6 in (15.24 mm) pitch through drilled holes. It can
also be modelled with straight leads cut short, for wiring in.
