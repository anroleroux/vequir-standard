# Chassis

The base of the robot, open at the top and along both long sides, carrying
the six-cell 18650 pack from the parts library, with a 2S BMS on the front of
the pack's mini wall, two IP6505 USB fast-charge boards and a CN3302 battery
charger, all behind plug holes in the front wall. The pack is screwed down
through its end strips' tabs into four pillars on the floor.
